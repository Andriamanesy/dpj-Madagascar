# Report a Case

Your safety comes first. You may submit a report with minimal personal information. Messages received by the collective are handled with confidentiality and care.

{{< alert >}}
Do not put yourself at risk to collect evidence. If you are monitored or threatened, use a safer channel and seek help from a trusted person.
{{< /alert >}}

## Useful Information

- What happened?
- Where and when did it happen?
- Who are the victims or witnesses, if this can be shared safely?
- Are there photos, videos, medical documents, or complaint references?

Before sending files, consider checking whether they contain sensitive metadata, such as GPS location, device name, or the identity of an exposed person.
