# Report a Case

Your safety comes first. You may submit a report with minimal personal information. Messages received by the collective are handled with confidentiality and care.

{{< alert >}}
Do not put yourself at risk to collect evidence. If you are monitored or threatened, use a safer channel and seek help from a trusted person.
{{< /alert >}}

## Useful Information

- Your name or pseudonym, if you wish to share it
- A safe contact method, if you agree to be contacted
- What happened
- Where and when it happened
- Victims or witnesses, only if this can be shared safely
- Photos, videos, medical documents, complaint references, or other available material
