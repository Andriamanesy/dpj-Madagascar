# About Stand Up for Justice Madagascar

Stand Up for Justice Madagascar is an independent citizen collective dedicated to monitoring, documenting, and denouncing serious human rights violations.

In a context of institutional violence and persistent impunity, we act as a vigilant observer. Our mission is to document abuses, support victims, and engage national and international authorities.

## Governance

The collective is notably represented by Dr. MAMILAZA Novario and Mr. ANDRIAMANESY Brian.

## Organizations Contacted

Depending on each case, the collective may send correspondence or alerts to national and international bodies, including OHCHR, Amnesty International, ONDH, or relevant public authorities.

Mentioning an organization does not necessarily imply an official partnership.
