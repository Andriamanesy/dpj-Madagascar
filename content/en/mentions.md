# Ethical Notice

Stand Up for Justice Madagascar is an independent citizen collective. Information published on this site is part of citizen documentation, monitoring, and public engagement.

{{< alert >}}
Our publications do not replace a judicial investigation, a court decision, or legal representation. They aim to alert, document, and request that serious facts be examined by competent authorities.
{{< /alert >}}

## Documentary Caution

When information is not fully established, it is presented as allegations, testimonies, or elements requiring verification. The collective seeks to avoid unverified accusations and unnecessary exposure of victims, witnesses, or families.

## Protection of People

We may anonymize information, delay publication, or remove sensitive details when the safety of an exposed person requires it.

## Accountability

Any person mentioned or institution concerned may send response, clarification, or correction elements to: [deboutpourlajustice2@gmail.com](mailto:deboutpourlajustice2@gmail.com).
