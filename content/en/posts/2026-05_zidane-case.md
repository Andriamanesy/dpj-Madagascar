## Summary

Zidane, an 18-year-old young man, reportedly died while in custody in Ambanja. Testimonies and material elements refer to serious violence before his death.

These elements require an independent and impartial investigation consistent with international standards applicable to deaths in custody.

## Actions Taken

- Submissions to national and international human rights organizations.
- Letters addressed to relevant authorities.
- Request for an independent investigation based on the principles of the Minnesota Protocol.

## Requests

- Immediate opening of an independent investigation.
- Protection of relatives, witnesses, and people who provided information.
- Preservation of evidence and clarification of the chain of responsibility.
- Public communication on judicial or administrative follow-up.

## Current Status

The case is being actively monitored. Responses from authorities and contacted institutions are pending.
